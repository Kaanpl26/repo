# Case 1 Walkthrough (full spoilers)

Use this when testing. The Hint buttons in the game lead through the same steps.

## 1. Lobby
1. Collect: visitor log (book on the desk), patrol log (clipboard), staff portraits (board), newspaper (rack by the door), betting slip (bin), key-card log (card reader by the right-hand door).
2. Talk to Veysel Erdem. Ask about last night, then about his nine o'clock patrol. When he says he was in B2, tap **Present Evidence** and show the **Betting Slip**.
3. He unlocks the computer on his desk: collect the **car park barrier log**.

## 2. Vural's office (Map)
Collect: email (monitor), digitisation notes (notebook), calendar note, Aras's letter (letter tray), forced drawers.
Talk to Leyla Aydın. Ask when she left, then present the **Key-Card Log**. Choose the kind reply to get her full statement (the harsh reply still works later, after you find the stopped watch).

## 3. Director's office (Map)
Collect: diploma and certificate (frames), master key register (key cabinet).
- Dr. Vardar: ask where she was, present the **Car Park Barrier Log**. Ask about the email, present the **Sent Email**. Ask about 1998, present the **Diploma**.
- When she asks whether Vural left a note, **do not tell her** about the calendar note until you have found the file. Telling her first leads to the weaker ending.
- Demir Aras: ask about his visit, present his **Letter**.

## 4. Stack B2 (Map, opens after visiting Vural's office)
Collect: personal effects (tray), coroner's note (clipboard), jade earring (under the low shelf by the stairs), empty box 47-B.

## 5. The Board
Connect (the Board's Hint button shows valid pairs):
- Key-Card Log + Car Park Barrier Log
- Visitor Log + Morning Newspaper
- Key-Card Log + each of Erdem's three later statements
- Personal Effects + Coroner's Note
- Forced Drawers + Personal Effects
- Digitisation Notes + Diploma
- Sent Email + "The Director came back"
- Leyla's full statement + Master Key Register
- Jade Earring + Staff Portraits
- Calendar Note + Empty Box 47-B, which reveals the file's hiding place

## 6. The file
Return to Stack B2 and take **file 47-B** from the box marked 74-B (right-hand shelves).

## 7. Accusation (Case File, Suspects)
Accuse **Dr. Selin Vardar** with:
- Motive: "S.M. is Selin Maden"
- Opportunity: "The Director came back" (or the email or elevator conclusion)
- Physical link: "The earring is Dr. Vardar's"

## Endings
| Ending | How |
|---|---|
| The Mirror Tells the Truth (true ending) | Vardar, all three proofs correct, file found intact |
| Reasonable Doubt | Vardar with missing or wrong proof, or the file missing or damaged |
| The Obvious Man | Accuse Demir Aras |
| The Easy Answer | Accuse Leyla Aydın or Veysel Erdem |
| Misadventure | Close the case as an accident |

# Case 2 Walkthrough — "Dosya 58-D" (full spoilers)

Case 2 opens once Case 1 has reached any verdict.

## 1. Harbour Road
1. Talk to Emniyet Müdürü Soykan. When he suggests bringing in the site manager, answering **"Not until I have seen the truck for myself"** keeps a useful witness talking later.
2. Collect: traffic report (clipboard on the patrol car), phone, key with tag 117, visitor pass and crash recorder (all on the table), paint on the rear bumper of the wreck.
3. Case File, **Database**: look up the phone number, the company and Bora Kaplan.

## 2. Newsroom (Map)
Collect: sign-in sheet (by the door), switchboard log, the empty desk, advertising report (in the glass office), archive drawer index (plate above the drawers).
- Deniz Aktaş: ask when she came in, then present the **Staff Sign-in Sheet**. Choosing to keep it out of your report gets the laptop and what she saw straight away.

## 3. Building Site (Map)
Collect: gate log and camera still (gate hut), the pickup, incident book, concrete tickets.
- Database: look up the dark red saloon.
- Bora Kaplan: ask whether he saw anyone at the gate.

## 4. Planning Office (Map)
Collect: file register (on the stand), desk calendar.
- Tarık Güven: ask whether he knew Kerem, then present his **Desk Calendar**.

## 5. The Board
Connect (the Board's Hint button shows valid pairs):
- Traffic Report + Crash Recorder
- Paint + The Site Pickup
- Gate Log + Concrete Tickets
- The Reporter's Phone + Subscriber record
- Switchboard Log + Güven's Desk Calendar
- Gate Camera Still + Vehicle Record
- Paint + Vehicle Record
- The Empty Desk + Sign-in Sheet
- Key with Tag 117 + Archive Drawer Index, which opens drawer 117
- Kerem's Draft + what Deniz saw
- Kerem's Draft + File 58-D
- Trade Registry + File 58-D

## 6. The file
Return to the newsroom and open **drawer 117** in the archive on the right.

## 7. Accusation
Accuse **Nilgün Soylu** with:
- Motive: "The reporter he was about to name"
- Opportunity: "Her car was waiting for him" (or "One caller, two calls")
- Physical link: "Her car hit his"

## Endings
| Ending | How |
|---|---|
| Spiked (true ending) | Soylu, all three proofs correct, file 58-D found |
| No Comment | Soylu with missing or wrong proof, or without the file |
| The Obvious Man, Again | Accuse Bora Kaplan |
| The Wrong Deadline | Accuse Tarık Güven or Deniz Aktaş |
| Rain and Speed | Close the case as an accident |

# Case 3 — File 12-C (spoilers)

**Killer:** Rıza Bulut, retired harbour policeman. He put twenty crushed Digoksin tablets, collected on his dead wife's prescription, into the rakı he shared with Dr. Erol on Thursday night.

**Shortest route to the true ending (A, “Fresh Water”)**

1. House: speak to Hatice, collect all eight items.
2. Case File → Database: look up the 77 31 caller, the parking ticket, then Bulut's calls.
3. Institute: collect the four items; ask Dr. Nur when he last saw Dr. Erol and present the **Desk Diary**. He hands over **File 12-C**. Choose **“The copy stays here”** (reporting him spoils the file and caps the verdict at ending B).
4. Database: search the pharmacy.
5. Records room: register and personnel card. If you send the chief your notes before visiting the harbour, the blister packs in the stove will be ash.
6. Harbour: tally slate, payment slips, newspapers, stove.
7. Board: Motive = *Paid since the month the watchman died* (File 12-C + Payment Slips). Opportunity = *He was in the upper town* (Tally Slate + Bulut's Call Record) or *The fish came from shed No. 7*. Link = *The tablets were his wife's* (Rakı Analysis + Pharmacy Record) or *Twenty empty pockets*.
8. Case File → accuse Rıza Bulut with those three conclusions.

**Endings:** A full proof with the file; B a proof missing or the file reported through the chief; C accusing Dr. Nur; D accusing Cem or Hatice; E signing it as a natural death.

**Carried forward:** whether Nur was trusted, whether the chief got your notes, how you left Hatice, and whether you saw the chief's name in the file and the register.

# Case 4 — File 103-F (spoilers)

**The name to give the prosecutor:** Emniyet Müdürü Halil Soykan. He authorised every payment since 1998 and ordered Dr. Vardar's death; prison officer Kenan Yıldız was the hand.

**Shortest route to the true ending (A, “Under the Lamp”)**

1. Prison: speak to Savcı Arıkan, collect all six items. Database: run the three searches.
2. Law office: plate, key board, appointment book, agreement. Board: connect **Her Unsent Letter + Key Board** to put the lighthouse on the map.
3. Chief's office: car log, photograph, transfer order. When he asks where you are going, answer **“The prosecutor has the file”**. If you tell him, the ledger is burnt before you reach it and the best verdict is ending B.
4. Lighthouse: ledger, torn page, tin box, petrol can.
5. Board: Motive = *She was going to give them his name* or *The name on the page*. Opportunity = *He was in the building* (Visitors' Book + Official Car Log). Link = *He switched the camera off himself* or *The order to be alone with her*.
6. Case File → give the prosecutor Halil Soykan with those three conclusions.

**Endings:** A full proof with the ledger intact; B a proof missing or the ledger burnt; C Nedim Karaca takes the fall; D Yıldız or Aras; E signing the prison's report.
