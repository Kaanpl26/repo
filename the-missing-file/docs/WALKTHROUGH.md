# Case 1 Walkthrough (full spoilers)

Use this when testing. The Hint buttons in the game lead through the same steps.

## 1. Lobby
1. Collect: visitor log (book on the desk), patrol log (clipboard), staff portraits (board), newspaper (rack by the door), betting slip (bin), key-card log (card reader by the right-hand door).
2. Talk to Veysel Erdem. Ask about last night, then about his nine o'clock patrol. When he says he was in B2, tap **Present Evidence** and show the **Betting Slip**.
3. He unlocks the computer on his desk: collect the **car park barrier log**.

## 2. Vural's office (Map)
Collect: email (monitor), digitisation notes (notebook), calendar note, Aras's letter (letter tray), forced drawers.
Talk to Leyla Aydın. Ask when she left, then present the **Key-Card Log**. Choose the kind reply to get her full statement (the harsh reply still works later, after you find the stopped watch).

## 3. Director's office (Map)
Collect: diploma and certificate (frames), master key register (key cabinet).
- Dr. Vardar: ask where she was, present the **Car Park Barrier Log**. Ask about the email, present the **Sent Email**. Ask about 1998, present the **Diploma**.
- When she asks whether Vural left a note, **do not tell her** about the calendar note until you have found the file. Telling her first leads to the weaker ending.
- Demir Aras: ask about his visit, present his **Letter**.

## 4. Stack B2 (Map, opens after visiting Vural's office)
Collect: personal effects (tray), coroner's note (clipboard), jade earring (under the low shelf by the stairs), empty box 47-B.

## 5. The Board
Connect (the Board's Hint button shows valid pairs):
- Key-Card Log + Car Park Barrier Log
- Visitor Log + Morning Newspaper
- Key-Card Log + each of Erdem's three later statements
- Personal Effects + Coroner's Note
- Forced Drawers + Personal Effects
- Digitisation Notes + Diploma
- Sent Email + "The Director came back"
- Leyla's full statement + Master Key Register
- Jade Earring + Staff Portraits
- Calendar Note + Empty Box 47-B, which reveals the file's hiding place

## 6. The file
Return to Stack B2 and take **file 47-B** from the box marked 74-B (right-hand shelves).

## 7. Accusation (Case File, Suspects)
Accuse **Dr. Selin Vardar** with:
- Motive: "S.M. is Selin Maden"
- Opportunity: "The Director came back" (or the email or elevator conclusion)
- Physical link: "The earring is Dr. Vardar's"

## Endings
| Ending | How |
|---|---|
| The Mirror Tells the Truth (true ending) | Vardar, all three proofs correct, file found intact |
| Reasonable Doubt | Vardar with missing or wrong proof, or the file missing or damaged |
| The Obvious Man | Accuse Demir Aras |
| The Easy Answer | Accuse Leyla Aydın or Veysel Erdem |
| Misadventure | Close the case as an accident |
