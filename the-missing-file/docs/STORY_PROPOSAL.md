# Season Story Proposal — "The Lighthouse Ledger" / "Fener Defteri"

**Status:** proposal, awaiting owner approval. No content from Cases 2 to 4 has been written.
**Spoilers:** this document reveals the intended season ending.

---

## 1. Where Case 1 left things

- File 47-B (the 1998 Pier 9 warehouse fire) proves Aras Denizcilik paid a records clerk to retype a witness statement.
- A routing slip inside it lists six more files in the same hand: **12-C, 31-A, 58-D, 66-B, 90-A, 103-F**. None are on their shelves.
- One page was torn out long ago. The stub reads: **"Payment authorised by —"**.
- The night watchman whose statement was altered is never seen in Case 1.
- Already in the game since version 1.1.0: when pressed, Demir Aras names his lawyer, **Nedim Karaca**. This is the only season element already written. If the lawyer is rejected, it is a two-line change.

## 2. The season in one paragraph

In 1998 someone in Karaliman built a quiet service: for a fee, an inconvenient official record could be corrected. Each correction needed a clerk, an official signature and someone to authorise the payment. The clerks and officials changed; the person authorising did not. The six file numbers are six corrections. Four cases recover them, and each one brings Mira closer to the name on the torn page.

## 3. The two antagonists

### Visible: Av. Nedim Karaca, lawyer
- Appears in every case, always for the person Mira is closing in on, always already briefed.
- Polite, exact, never raises his voice. He is the reason weak cases fall apart: in interviews he cuts off questions that are not backed by a conclusion.
- Game role: from Case 2 onward, a suspect with Karaca present has lower patience and some statements can only be challenged with a Board conclusion, not raw evidence.
- He is not the final culprit. He is paid from the same ledger and knows it.

### Hidden: the person who authorised the payments
Three candidates. I recommend the first.

| Candidate | Why it works | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Emniyet Müdürü Halil Soykan**, Mira's own chief (recommended) | In 1998 he was the young inspector who signed off the fire inquiry. He assigned Mira to Case 1 expecting a quick "accident" ruling from a newcomer. Every case, her own reports have been going to him. | The twist is a familiar one in the genre, so it must be planted fairly from Case 2. |
| B. Dr. İdris Nur, the coroner | He has been a friendly voice since Case 1, and Case 3 turns on an old inquest. | He was too young in 1998 to be the authoriser without stretching the timeline. Better as a red herring in Case 3. |
| C. A retired mayor, off-stage until the finale | Clean and uncontroversial. | No personal stake for the player; the reveal lands on a stranger. |

With candidate A the season needs one new ally so that Mira has someone to report to at the end: **Savcı Zeynep Arıkan**, the prosecutor who receives file 47-B after Case 1.

## 4. The four cases

Four cases cover six files: Cases 2 and 3 recover one each, and the finale recovers the remaining three together with the ledger itself.

### Case 1 — "Dosya 47-B" (done)
The archive. Recovers 47-B.

### Case 2 — "Dosya 58-D": the journalist
- **File:** a 2004 zoning ruling that turned the burnt Pier 9 land into building land. It is the reason the fire was worth setting.
- **Victim:** Kerem Tunç, the journalist who was paying Leyla Aydın for copies of zoning files in Case 1. Found in his car on the harbour road; it looks like a crash in the rain. His notes are gone.
- **Places:** the newspaper office, the harbour road, the municipal planning office, a half-built tower on the waterfront.
- **Suspects (four):** his editor, a city planner, the site manager of Demir Aras's waterfront project, and a rival reporter.
- **New mechanic:** the police database. Number plates, phone records and company registrations are looked up and turned into cards.
- **Carried from Case 1:**
  - If the guard was spared, Veysel Erdem phones with what he saw from the night desk.
  - If Leyla was treated kindly, she comes forward on her own as the victim's source; otherwise she has to be found.
  - If Aras admitted trying to buy the file, Karaca is already prepared for it.
- **Thread:** the planner's approval was "corrected" by the same hand. A payment reference in the journalist's notes matches the format of the torn stub.

### Case 3 — "Dosya 12-C": the inquest
- **File:** the 1999 inquest into the death of the Pier 9 night watchman, the witness from page 12. Ruled an accidental drowning.
- **Victim:** the retired coroner who signed that inquest, found dead at home the week he agreed to speak to the prosecutor.
- **Places:** the forensic institute, the victim's house, the old harbour, the police records room.
- **Suspects (four):** the victim's former assistant, his son, a retired harbour policeman, and Dr. İdris Nur, who trained under him.
- **Twist of the case:** the evidence points at Dr. Nur for most of it. He is hiding something, but it is that he copied the old inquest file years ago because he never believed it.
- **Thread:** Dr. Nur's copy shows the investigating officer's signature on the 1999 inquest. The same officer signed the 1998 fire inquiry. This is the first time the player can see the chief's name on paper, if they look.

### Case 4 — "Dosya 103-F": the ledger
- **File:** 103-F is the municipal finance file that records the "consultancy" payments. With it are 31-A, 66-B and 90-A.
- **Victim:** Dr. Selin Vardar, days before she is due to testify. If Case 1 ended with her fully convicted she is attacked in custody; the investigation is the same either way.
- **Places:** the remand prison visiting room, Karaca's law office, the chief's office, the old lighthouse where the ledger has been kept.
- **Suspects (four):** Nedim Karaca, Demir Aras, a prison officer, and Emniyet Müdürü Halil Soykan.
- **Structure:** the final accusation is made to the prosecutor, not to Mira's own chain of command. Reporting progress to the chief during the case is a choice, and it costs evidence.
- **Endings:** the authoriser exposed with the ledger intact; exposed but the ledger burnt; Karaca takes the fall; the case is buried and Mira is transferred.

## 5. Cross-case "Ledger" board

In the detective office (expansion phase 5) a second board holds one card per recovered file plus a few carried cards (the routing slip, the torn stub, the payment reference, the two signatures). It uses the same connect-two-cards rule as the case board. Its conclusions are what make the true ending of Case 4 reachable, so solving the season is itself a deduction, not only the last case.

## 6. Fair-play rules for the season

- The chief appears in person from Case 2, behaves helpfully, and is never shown doing anything the player could not later explain.
- Every case can be solved without having played the others; carried decisions change who helps and how easy it is, never whether it can be solved.
- Each case exports at most three carried decisions.
- Karaca never lies to the player. He refuses, deflects and ends interviews.
